Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-the-most-boring-object-reviewed-in-one-line-bd3117c3): one funny, true line reviewing the dullest everyday object we could name.

## The entry

> Six years of faithful service, and the rubber band's retirement plan was snapping my wrist in a meeting.

104 characters, one line. The rubber band is the dullest thing on any desk — no moving parts, no purpose beyond holding other things together, invisible until the single moment it fails. The joke comes from that truth: a lifetime of quiet, faithful service, repaid with one sting on the wrist in the middle of a meeting.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no dependencies) composes the review from its two beats — the setup (loyal service) and the punch (the snap) — validates it against the gate (one non-blank line, 1–200 characters), and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Running it twice produces byte-identical output (sha256 `a606f4d3e02ea09f5968732dc73da43e4d2c329c0230adefdf24334e5c2e4681`).
